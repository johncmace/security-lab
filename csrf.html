Web security · OWASP A01 · CWE-352

Cross-site request forgery, X-rayed

You're logged into your bank. You open an unrelated page in another tab. That page quietly fires a request back to the bank — and because your browser attaches your session cookie to every request to that site, the bank can't tell the difference between you and the attacker's page. Watch the forged request go through, then add the defences that stop it.

Vulnerable: no CSRF token, and the session cookie is sent on cross-site requests.
