Web security · OWASP A07 · CWE-287

Broken authentication, X-rayed

When you log in, the server hands your browser a session cookie that keeps you logged in. If that cookie encodes who you are — your id, your name, a hash of it — instead of being a random handle the server looks up, then anyone can edit their own cookie and become someone else. Forge an admin session, then switch on tokens that can't be forged.

Vulnerable: the cookie encodes your identity, and the server trusts whatever it says.
